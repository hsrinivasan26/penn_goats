How it works

Five years, one month at a time. Each month:

1
A paycheck arrives
See what's really left after taxes — take-home, not the offer.
2
Essentials come out
Rent, food, transport, utilities are paid automatically.
3
Life happens
A surprise hits — a repair, a bonus, sometimes a layoff.
4
You choose
Invest, pay down debt, treat yourself, or make a big move.

Reach your net-worth goal by month 60 to win. Run out of cash for too long, or let happiness hit zero, and it's over. Watch your four rings:

Cash — money on hand Investments — grows or shrinks Debt — what you owe Happiness — 0 = burnout
